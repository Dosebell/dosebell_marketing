Visit support ↗
Is DoseBell a free pill reminder app?

Medication reminders, all schedule types, dose history, refill tracking, widgets, and Apple Watch support are free. DoseBell Pro adds profiles, caregiver sharing, PDF reports, label scanning, and personalisation with a one-time purchase.

Does DoseBell work offline?

Yes. You can add medications, log doses, and receive already-scheduled reminders without a connection. iCloud sync, purchases, and caregiver updates need connectivity. Open the app regularly to refresh its reminder schedule.

Will reminders sound when my phone is on silent?

Standard reminders respect Silent mode. Time-sensitive notifications can appear through some Focus settings when you allow them. Critical Alerts require a separate Apple entitlement and your permission; they are not available in this release.

What can my caregiver see?

Only the status updates you choose to share through an invitation: today’s progress, weekly adherence, and missed-dose updates. Medication names are optional. Your full medication database stays private. Updates can be delayed when devices are offline or inactive.

Can DoseBell tell me how much medication to take?

No. DoseBell records your prescribed schedule and your entries. It does not provide dosage advice or check interactions. Follow your prescriber’s instructions.
